Improving Medication Access for Seniors

Emergency Call

During emergencies, timely medical assistance can save lives. It's vital that seniors and caregivers have quick access to emergency support.

Medicine Schedule

Maintaining a consistent medication routine is crucial for managing chronic conditions. Example weekly schedule:

  • Monday – Aspirin 75mg
  • Tuesday – Metformin 500mg
  • Wednesday – Atorvastatin 10mg
  • Thursday – Lisinopril 5mg
  • Friday – Levothyroxine 50mcg
  • Saturday – Amlodipine 5mg
  • Sunday – Vitamin D3

Subsidized Medication Programs

NGOs and government initiatives help reduce medication costs. Volunteers can connect eligible seniors to these programs.

  • Helping Hands Foundation
  • Care4Seniors Initiative
  • Global Health Relief
  • PharmaAid Network

Home Prescription Delivery

Home delivery helps seniors who live far from pharmacies. Volunteers can help coordinate deliveries.

Digital Pill Reminders

Apps and pill dispensers can be used to reduce missed doses and overdosing risk.

Medication Reviews

Regular reviews by pharmacists or doctors reduce harmful interactions and focus on necessary medications only.

Local Support Networks

Community health workers and volunteers can assist with prescriptions, pillboxes, and interpreting instructions.
